China travel FAQ for first-time visitors

Short, sourced answers to the questions people ask before and during a first trip to China. Visa and entry decisions are made only by immigration authorities; this is planning information, not legal advice.

Is China safe?

Main-street safety in major Chinese cities is on the good side globally, and violent crime against tourists is rare. What actually catches visitors out isn’t danger — it’s scams.

Basis: MPS public security guidance and travel advisories for major Chinese cities, 2026.

Will my phone work? Google / WhatsApp?

Phone and app availability depends on your device, operator, plan and network. Do not assume Google, WhatsApp or any other overseas app will work on every connection.

Maintenance note: general connectivity preparation, not live network testing or a verified operator quotation. Confirm current terms and app availability with your provider.

I don’t speak Chinese.

Language support varies by venue and staff. Before booking, ask your hotel, transport provider and attractions which languages and assistance they currently offer.

General language preparation advice, not a verified inventory of devices or multilingual services. Confirm current support with each provider.

How do I pay? Is cash still usable?

Prepare more than one payment method and confirm availability before travelling. Card linking, identity checks and merchant acceptance depend on the provider and your account.

General payment preparation advice, not a verified fee schedule or acceptance guarantee. Confirm current terms with the wallet provider, merchant and issuing bank.

How much will it cost?

Build a budget using quotes for your dates and itinerary. Flights, accommodation, transport, meals and activities vary by provider and booking conditions.

General budgeting preparation, not verified prices or fees. Confirm current quotes and terms directly with providers.

Do I need a visa?

Check your passport, purpose, dates and full itinerary against the relevant official notice. A country list alone does not confirm eligibility. This tool covers selected routes only; missing coverage does not mean a visa is necessarily required.

Who do I call if something goes wrong?

Save emergency contacts, your accommodation details and provider support information before travelling. Language support and service availability may vary.
